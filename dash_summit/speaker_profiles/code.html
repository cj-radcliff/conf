The Future of Scalable UI

schedule 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM
location_on Room 201

About this session

Join Elara Vance, CEO of InnovateX, as she unveils cutting-edge strategies for building resilient and scalable user interfaces. This session will delve into component-driven architecture, state management patterns for large-scale applications, and performance optimization techniques that ensure a seamless user experience, no matter the complexity.

About the speaker

Elara Vance

CEO, InnovateX

Elara is a visionary leader with over 15 years of experience in the tech industry, specializing in frontend architecture and user experience design.
